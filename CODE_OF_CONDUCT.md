# Code of conduct

Flagnetic adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone participating in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel) is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults, and unwelcome sexual or discriminatory behaviour are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@azverinus](https://github.com/azverinus), through the contact on that profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers will review each report and act as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a private warning up to a permanent ban.
